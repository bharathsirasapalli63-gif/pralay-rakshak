Critical Situation Survival Guide & Safety Instructions

LIFE-SAVING PROTOCOLS

Authoritative emergency drills and survival techniques for Himalayan/Northeastern landslides and cloudbursts.

GOLDEN RULE: If you hear a loud rumbling sound like a freight train or see cracking slopes, act instantly without hesitation!
If You Are Indoors
  • Move to Upper Floor: Escape ground-floor rooms vulnerable to mud/debris impact.
  • Take Sturdy Cover: Get under a heavy wooden table, bed, or interior door frame.
  • Fetal Tuck Position: Curl into a tight ball, hands over head to protect skull & neck.
  • Stay Away from Windows: Downslope glass windows and exterior walls shatter first.
If You Are Outdoors
  • Run Perpendicular: Sprint sideways across the slope, NEVER straight downhill.
  • Seek High Solid Ground: Move away from stream channels, ravines, and gullies.
  • Avoid Weak Trees & Poles: Retaining walls and shallow trees collapse easily.
  • Protect Your Head: If debris traps you, roll into a tight ball and cover head.
If You Are in a Vehicle
  • Watch Road Shoulders: Stop before sunken pavement or active rockfall zones.
  • Abandon Stalled Vehicles: If highway shoulder collapses, get out and head uphill.
  • Never Cross Debris Flows: Fast-moving mud/boulders easily overturn SUVs & buses.
LANDSLIDE FOREWARNING SIGNS: Nature always gives visual and acoustic cues before major slope collapse.
Visual Ground Warnings
  • Tension cracks in soil, driveways, retaining walls, or house floors.
  • Telephone poles, fences, or tall pine trees tilting or leaning downslope.
  • Doors and window frames suddenly jamming or sticking due to foundation shift.
  • New muddy springs or water seepage emerging from the toe of the slope.
Family Disaster Action Plan
  • Identify 2 safe uphill evacuation routes to the nearest community shelter.
  • Save local DDMA/SDRF emergency numbers in speed dial and on paper.
  • Monitor the live early warning radar on this platform during heavy monsoon deluges.
Post-Slide Safety & Rescue
  • Stay Away from Slide Scar: Secondary slides and dammed flash floods occur frequently.
  • Assist Injured from Safe Distance: Direct NDRF/SDRF rescue teams rather than stepping on unstable debris.
  • Report Downed Utilities: Look out for broken electrical lines, gas leaks, and ruptured water pipes.
  • Check Platform Triage: Log your status and location on the Incident Report tab.
